Our Story

Cooked slow, served with care

We started in 2014 with one rule: if it isn't good enough for our own table, it doesn't leave the kitchen. Everything is made in-house — from the bread to the sauces to the desserts.

Our produce comes from three family farms within 50 miles, and our menu changes with the seasons because that's when food tastes best.
